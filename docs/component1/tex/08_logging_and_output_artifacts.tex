\section{Logging Contracts and Output Artifacts}
\label{sec:c1_outputs}

\subsection{Output directory layout}
For run split \texttt{split}, Component 1 writes under:
\begin{equation}
\texttt{<out\_dir>/<split>/}
\end{equation}
with default \texttt{out\_dir = logs/component1}.

Typical files:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{claims.jsonl} (always),
\item \texttt{pairs.jsonl} (when pair logging mode is not \texttt{none}),
\item \texttt{summary.json} (after summarizer script),
\item \texttt{examples/highest\_contradiction.json},
\item \texttt{examples/lowest\_esi.json},
\item \texttt{examples/most\_neutral.json}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Claim-level JSONL contract}
Each line in \texttt{claims.jsonl} is one full claim record.
Top-level fields:

\centering
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.24\linewidth}p{0.16\linewidth}p{0.54\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
Field & Type & Meaning \\
\midrule
\texttt{claim\_id} & string & Stable id \texttt{<split>\_<index>}. \\
\texttt{claim\_text} & string & Original claim sentence. \\
\texttt{label} & string & \texttt{"TRUE"} or \texttt{"FALSE"}. \\
\texttt{entity\_set} & list[string] & Canonical claim entities for downstream use. \\

\texttt{counts} & object & Cardinalities of \texttt{A/S/C}. \\
\texttt{sufficiency} & object & ESI and related metrics. \\
\texttt{counter\_retention} & object & CR metrics and contradiction leakage checks. \\
\texttt{recovery} & object & Bridge and RPI diagnostics. \\
\texttt{pool\_stats} & object & Distributional stats over full pool. \\
\texttt{top\_evidence} & object & Top ids by relevance and contradiction. \\
\texttt{starvation} & object & Legacy starvation indicators. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\subsection{Summary artifact contract}
\texttt{scripts/summarize\_component1.py} reads \texttt{claims.jsonl} and writes:
\begin{itemize}
\item aggregate means and percentiles,
\item starvation rates (fixed threshold and percentile threshold),
\item CR and RPI means,
\item example dumps sorted by contradiction, ESI, and neutral rate.
\end{itemize}
It uses \texttt{esi\_geom} as primary metric and falls back to \texttt{esi\_prod} for legacy logs.

\subsection{Compatibility guarantees for Component 2}
Component 1 logs include explicit compatibility fields consumed by Component 2 loaders:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{entity\_set} (and alias \texttt{Entity\_set}) in claim rows,
\item \texttt{pool} (and alias \texttt{evidence\_assignment}) in pair rows,
\item \texttt{raw\_triple} and \texttt{probs} fields for triple reconstruction and PV signal reuse.
\end{itemize}
